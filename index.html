X12 835 Helper

X12 835 Explainer

Turns a remittance advice into a readable document: the payment, then one record per claim with every adjustment explained and the balancing checked.

Private by design. Runs entirely in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, saved or sent anywhere.

Vendor profiles

Profiles add payer-specific meaning (reason-code descriptions, payer-specific qualifiers) on top of the standard. Anything a profile explains is tagged with its name.

The stub lists segments, field positions and codes this page could not explain. It never includes field contents.

Tip: ← → keys move between records
An explainer, not a validator. Its sister project, HL7-Helper, does the same for HL7 v2. Base dictionary: X12 005010X221 segment and element names with plain-English explanations written for this project. Claim adjustment reason codes (CARC), remark codes (RARC) and NUBC code descriptions are not included — load them through a vendor profile.
